Privacy Policy

Dig & Drip: Paint Puzzle · Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Dig & Drip app ("the app", "we") handles. The app is made by Hetansh Patel. If you have a question, email hetansh11@outlook.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone. We do not receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, outfits and settings) and simple play statistics (such as how many times you tried a level) on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is deleted if you delete the app. A tester can choose to share their play statistics as text through the iOS share sheet; nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

If you turn on tilt, the app reads your phone's motion sensors to steer the paint. This happens on your phone only and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show ads between levels and optional ads that reward a free hint. To serve, limit and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Device identifiers, such as an identifier for this app on your device
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • Product interaction and performance data, such as app launches and crash information

Because the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google, and ads are not personalised using it. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You can reset the advertising settings on your iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • You can delete all game data at any time by deleting the app.
  • For information Google holds, see My Ad Center and Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
