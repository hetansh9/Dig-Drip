I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap the light bulb at the top of the screen. "Where do I dig next?" shows the next tunnel; "Show me the whole path" shows the full solution. You start with free hints, get one free every day, and can earn more with coins or by watching a short ad.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app?

No. Updating keeps your levels, stars, coins and outfits. Deleting the app erases them, because progress is stored only on your phone.

The paint drifts to one side.

Tilting your phone steers the paint. You can switch tilt off on the world map (tap the level name at the top left, then "Tilt").

How often are there ads?

No ads in the first 11 levels. After that, at most one short ad every four levels. Ads for free hints are always your choice.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open the world map (tap the level name at the top left) and tap "Sound".

Privacy
